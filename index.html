Our story

The story behind
Biryani by Prince.

Founded by Prince Goyal, Biryani by Prince began with a vision: bring the rich taste and traditional essence of Hyderabadi Dum Biryani to food lovers, with quality, consistency and genuine hospitality at its heart.

What started as a small cloud-kitchen journey has grown into a brand with a loyal following and multiple kitchen operations in Jodhpur. Each pot is prepared with selected ingredients, balanced spices and the slow dum-cooking technique that creates our signature aroma.

“Serve great biryani, build genuine connections, and create a brand people proudly come back to.”— Prince Goyal, Founder & Owner
